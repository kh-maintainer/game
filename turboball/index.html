How to play

Knock the ball into your opponent's goal. Most goals when the clock runs out wins. If it's tied, overtime goes on until someone scores.

Controls

W / ↑Accelerate
S / ↓Brake / reverse
AD / ←→Steer
Shift / left mouseBoost
Space / right mouseHop (steer while hopping to side-flip, hold back to back-flip)
1–5Quick chat
EscPause / menu
GamepadRT/LT drive, stick steer, A hop, B/X/RB boost

Tips

  • Boost pads: big orange pads refill you to 100, small ones give +22. Boost resets to 33 at every kickoff.
  • Hop into the ball to pop it up and hit it much harder. Hop over your opponent's car to dodge a bump.
  • Hit the ball while boosting for extra power.
  • Get behind the ball before you shoot. Driving at it from the wrong side scores own goals.
  • Frostbite Rink is ice (you'll slide). Dune Bowl sand slows the ball.
  • A goal plays an instant replay. Press Space or Enter to skip it.
